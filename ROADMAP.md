## Open scope question for Phase 1
Research recommends narrowing Phases 4–8's *first pass* to a 2–3 domain wedge (documents/bills, home/warranties, vehicle) rather than the full 40+ entity model, to ship the cross-domain graph loop faster than Lifey/Babs can close the gap. See [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) and [docs/research/differentiation.md](docs/research/differentiation.md). This needs to be explicitly ratified or overridden when Phase 1 architecture is proposed.
